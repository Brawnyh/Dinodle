Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Dinodle (dinodle.app) is a free daily dinosaur guessing game run by an independent developer based in France. Contact: contact@dinodle.app.

What we collect

Dinodle has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal information to play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never sent to us:

  • today's game (your guesses, clues used and score), your statistics and game history;
  • your language and difficulty choice;
  • the list of dinosaurs you have found (used by the credits page);
  • addresses of dinosaur photos already found, so they load faster.

You can delete all of this at any time from your browser settings (clear site data for dinodle.app), or reset your statistics in the game.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Images: when a game ends, the dinosaur photo is loaded from Wikipedia / Wikimedia, which receives your IP address like any website you visit. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

Audience statistics

To know how many people play and which pages are visited, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not build a profile of you; it counts page views in aggregate (page, referring site, country, browser and device type). See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Donations

If you choose to support Dinodle, the payment happens on the donation platform (for example PayPal or Ko-fi), under its own privacy policy. We only receive what that platform shares with us about your donation.

Advertising and cookies

Dinodle is free and may show ads served by Google AdSense to cover its costs. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message (a Google-certified consent management platform) asks for your choice before personalised ads or cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy & cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page once ads are active.

Children

Dinodle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not store personal data on our side, most of this is in your hands through your browser. For any question, write to contact@dinodle.app. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
