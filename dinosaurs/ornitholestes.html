About Ornitholestes

Ornitholestes hermanni was a carnivorous theropod that lived during the Late Jurassic, roughly 150 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in North America. Scientists estimate it was about 2 metres long and weighed around 12 kg — about the weight of a medium-sized dog. The Late Jurassic was the age of giant sauropods, when the supercontinent Pangaea was breaking apart. As a meat-eater, it hunted or scavenged other animals. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #129 by length and #140 by weight. It was discovered by Henry F. Osborn in 1903, 123 years ago.
