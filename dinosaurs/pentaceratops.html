About Pentaceratops

Pentaceratops sternbergii was a herbivorous ceratopsian that lived during the Late Cretaceous, roughly 75 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in North America. Scientists estimate it was about 7 metres long and weighed around 4,000 kg — roughly the weight of 2.7 cars. The Late Cretaceous was the last chapter of the age of dinosaurs, which ended with an asteroid impact about 66 million years ago. As a plant-eater, it fed on the ferns, conifers and other vegetation of its time. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #64 by length and #34 by weight. It was discovered by Henry Fairfield Osborn in 1923, 103 years ago.
