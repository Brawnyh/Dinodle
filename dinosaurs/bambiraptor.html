About Bambiraptor

Bambiraptor feinbergi was a carnivorous theropod that lived during the Late Cretaceous, roughly 75 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in North America. Scientists estimate it was about 0.9 metres long and weighed around 2 kg — lighter than a house cat. The Late Cretaceous was the last chapter of the age of dinosaurs, which ended with an asteroid impact about 66 million years ago. As a meat-eater, it hunted or scavenged other animals. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #152 by length and #151 by weight. It was discovered by David Burnham in 2000, 26 years ago.
