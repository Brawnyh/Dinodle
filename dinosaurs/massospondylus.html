About Massospondylus

Massospondylus carinatus was a herbivorous sauropod that lived during the Early Jurassic, roughly 195 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in Africa. Scientists estimate it was about 5 metres long and weighed around 1,000 kg — about 14 times the weight of an adult human. In the Early Jurassic, dinosaurs became the dominant land animals after a mass extinction at the end of the Triassic. As a plant-eater, it fed on the ferns, conifers and other vegetation of its time. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #100 by length and #87 by weight. It was discovered by Richard Owen in 1854, 172 years ago.
