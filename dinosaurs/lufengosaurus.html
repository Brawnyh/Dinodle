About Lufengosaurus

Lufengosaurus huenei was a herbivorous sauropodomorph that lived during the Early Jurassic, roughly 195 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in Asia. Scientists estimate it was about 6 metres long and weighed around 1,500 kg — roughly the weight of 1 car. In the Early Jurassic, dinosaurs became the dominant land animals after a mass extinction at the end of the Triassic. As a plant-eater, it fed on the ferns, conifers and other vegetation of its time. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #79 by length and #74 by weight. It was discovered by C.C. Young in 1941, 85 years ago.
