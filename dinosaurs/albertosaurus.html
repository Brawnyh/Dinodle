About Albertosaurus

Albertosaurus sarcophagus was a carnivorous theropod that lived during the Late Cretaceous, roughly 70 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in North America. Scientists estimate it was about 9 metres long and weighed around 2,000 kg — roughly the weight of 1.3 cars. The Late Cretaceous was the last chapter of the age of dinosaurs, which ended with an asteroid impact about 66 million years ago. As a meat-eater, it hunted or scavenged other animals. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #36 by length and #65 by weight. It was discovered by Henry F. Osborn in 1905, 121 years ago.
