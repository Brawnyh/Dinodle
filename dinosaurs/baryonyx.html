About Baryonyx

Baryonyx walkeri was a carnivorous theropod that lived during the Early Cretaceous, roughly 125 million years ago. Its fossils have been found in Europe. Scientists estimate it was about 8 metres long and weighed around 1,700 kg — roughly the weight of 1.1 cars. In the Early Cretaceous, the first flowering plants appeared and the continents kept drifting apart. As a meat-eater, it hunted or scavenged other animals. Among the 155 dinosaurs in Dinodle, it ranks #51 by length and #73 by weight. It was discovered by William Walker in 1983, 43 years ago.
