Frequently asked questions

How do I play Dinodle?
Type the name of any dinosaur and press Enter. Each guess shows how it compares with the secret dinosaur of the day: period, diet, group, weight, length and continent. Green means a match, yellow means close and grey means no match. Arrows tell you if the secret dinosaur is heavier or longer (↑), lighter or shorter (↓), older (←) or more recent (→). You have 12 attempts.
What is the difference between Easy and Normal mode?
Easy mode only uses the 25 most famous dinosaurs, such as Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops and Stegosaurus, and gives you bones sooner. Normal mode uses all 155 dinosaurs in the game. Each mode has its own daily puzzle and its own statistics, so you can play both every day.
What are bones and clues?
You dig up bones 🦴 as you play: at attempts 3, 6 and 9 in Normal mode (2, 4 and 6 in Easy). Each clue costs one bone. Clues reveal who discovered the dinosaur, a short description and, as the very last clue, its silhouette.
How is my score calculated?
You start with 1000 points. You lose 100 points every 3 attempts and 150 points for each bone you spend on a clue. If you do not find the dinosaur within 12 attempts, the score is 0. Your streak continues as long as you play every day.
When does the daily dinosaur change?
A new secret dinosaur appears every day at midnight, Paris time (Central European Time), and it is the same for every player in the world. A countdown at the end of the game shows how long until the next one.
Which languages is Dinodle available in?
Dinodle is available in English, French and Spanish. The game picks your browser language automatically, and you can switch at any time with the language buttons at the top of the game. The dinosaur fact pages also exist in all three languages.
How do I share my result?
When you finish, press the Share button. It copies a grid of coloured squares with the puzzle number, so your friends can see how you did without the answer being spoiled.
Where does the dinosaur information come from?
Facts such as period, diet, size and discovery are compiled from public scientific sources and Wikipedia, and rounded to keep the game fair. Size and weight estimates vary between studies. Silhouettes come from PhyloPic artists and are credited on the image credits page.
Do I need an account? Is my data stored?
No account is needed and Dinodle is free. Your games, statistics and settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). See the privacy page for details.
How can I contact you or suggest a dinosaur?
Write to contact@dinodle.app with ideas, bug reports, corrections or dinosaurs you would like to see in the game.
