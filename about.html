About Dinodle

What is Dinodle?

Dinodle is a free daily guessing game for dinosaur fans. Every day at midnight (Paris time) a new secret dinosaur appears, the same for everyone. You guess any dinosaur and the game tells you how close you are: did it live in the same period? Did it eat the same food? Was it heavier or longer, and on which continent was it found?

How to play

  • Green means a match, yellow means close, grey means no match. Arrows show if the secret dinosaur is bigger ↑, smaller ↓, older ← or more recent →.
  • You earn bones 🦴 as you play. Spend them on clues: who discovered it, a short description, and finally its silhouette.
  • You have 12 attempts. Fewer attempts and fewer bones mean a higher score. Share your result with friends without spoiling the answer.
  • Easy mode only uses the 25 most famous dinosaurs — perfect to start. Normal mode uses every dinosaur in the game.

Where does the information come from?

Dinosaur facts (period, diet, size, discovery) are compiled from public scientific sources and Wikipedia, and rounded to keep the game fair. Estimates of weight and length vary between studies, so if you spot a mistake, please tell us. Silhouettes are by the artists of PhyloPic — see the image credits.

Who makes it?

Dinodle is made by an independent developer in France who loves dinosaurs and daily puzzles. It is free to play, works on phones and computers, and is available in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German and Italian.
